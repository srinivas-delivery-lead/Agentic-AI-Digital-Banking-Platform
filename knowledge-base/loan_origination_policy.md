# Synthetic Loan Origination Policy

This policy is fictional and for portfolio demonstration only.

A loan application passes through KYC, eligibility, risk assessment and human approval. KYC must be VERIFIED. The demo eligibility rule requires monthly income of at least INR 50,000 and requested amount no more than 12 times monthly income. The demo risk agent uses declared obligations and application attributes only. The AI must never represent its output as a real credit decision. Final approval/rejection is a human-controlled action.
